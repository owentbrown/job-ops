Where the switcher lives

1 · PRIMARY
Sidebar footer: “Appearance”
Always visible on every page, just above Collapse. Shows the current choice (“Editorial · Teal”) so it’s discoverable. Opens a small popover with mode, theme and accent, previewing live. When the sidebar is collapsed it shrinks to the palette icon. On narrow screens it sits at the bottom of the drawer.
2 · QUICK TOGGLE
Sun / moon button in the page header
One click flips light and dark, because that is the change people make most often (evening job-hunting). It stays in the top-right of every page, beside the range picker or Add a job.
3 · FULL HOME
Settings → Appearance
The same controls with larger theme previews, plus the “Match my device” explanation. The popover links here. First run: pick a theme during onboarding, defaulting to Editorial · Teal · System.

Three personalities, what actually changes

Each theme changes type, shape, sidebar, tile treatment, density and the status bar. The colour pair then paints whichever surfaces that theme exposes.
